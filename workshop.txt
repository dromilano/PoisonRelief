title=Gastro-Calm & Herbal Tablets [B42]
description=[h1]Gastro-Calm & Herbal Tablets[/h1]
description=[b]For Project Zomboid Build 42.[/b]
description=
description=Adds two ways to treat Food Sickness and poisoning without turning either into an instant non-event. Each medicine gives limited immediate relief, then continues working over time.
description=
description=[h2]Treatments[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Gastro-Calm Tablets[/b] - Factory-made medicine found in medical loot and occasionally in bathroom counters. Each blister contains ten doses. One dose applies 10 relief immediately, then 25 more over 45 in-game minutes (35 total).
description=[*][b]Crude Herbal Tablets[/b] - Crafted from lemongrass with a mortar and pestle. One tablet applies 7 relief immediately, then 15 more over one in-game hour (22 total).
description=[/list]
description=
description=Relief lowers both Food Sickness and Poison. Additional doses can bank progressive relief, up to a maximum of 70 points. The fastest active medicine determines the treatment rate.
description=
description=Take either medicine directly from your inventory, a carried bag, an accessible loot container, or the floor. Gastro-Calm returns to its original container with one fewer dose; a Crude Herbal Tablet is consumed completely.
description=
description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=Use a mortar and pestle with two units of lemongrass to make four Crude Herbal Tablets. The mortar and pestle is kept and may take light wear.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=Supported. Medicine use is validated and processed by the server, with treatment state synchronized to the player.
description=
description=[h2]Important[/h2]
description=These tablets reduce Food Sickness and Poison. They do not cure zombie infection, wound infection, colds, or directly restore ordinary health.
description=
description=[b]No dependencies.[/b]
description=Mod ID: PoisonRelief
