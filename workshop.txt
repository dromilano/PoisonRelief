title=Poison Relief - Gastro-Calm & Herbal Tablets [B42]
description=[h1]Poison Relief[/h1]
description=[b]Lore-friendly treatment for Food Sickness and Poison in Project Zomboid Build 42.[/b]
description=
description=When bad food - or something worse - starts fighting back, Poison Relief gives you two ways to treat it: factory-made Gastro-Calm to scavenge and weaker herbal tablets to craft.
description=
description=Both medicines provide immediate relief, then continue working over time. They can save a survivor, but they are not an instant cure - and every dose makes you thirstier.
description=
description=[h2]Two Treatments[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Gastro-Calm Tablets[/b] - Factory-made medicine found in medical loot and, rarely, bathroom counters. Each blister contains ten doses. One dose applies 10 relief immediately, then 25 more over 45 in-game minutes (35 total). Causes significant thirst.
description=[*][b]Crude Herbal Tablet[/b] - A weaker alternative crafted from lemongrass with a mortar and pestle. One tablet applies 7 relief immediately, then 15 more over one in-game hour (22 total). Causes thirst.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Each point of relief lowers both Food Sickness and Poison. Gradual relief from additional doses can be banked up to 70 points, and the fastest active medicine determines the recovery rate.
description=
description=[b]Keep water nearby.[/b] Thirst stacks with every dose. Taking a whole fresh Gastro-Calm blister without drinking will push Thirst to maximum. You may cure one problem and manufacture another with admirable efficiency.
description=
description=[h2]Use Medicine Where You Find It[/h2]
description=Take either medicine directly from your main inventory, a carried bag, an accessible loot container, or the floor.
description=
description=A Gastro-Calm blister returns to its original container with one fewer dose. A Crude Herbal Tablet is consumed completely.
description=
description=[h2]Loot and Crafting[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Gastro-Calm can appear in medical-clinic drug storage, medical storage, and rarely in bathroom counters.
description=[*]Use two units of lemongrass with a mortar and pestle to craft four Crude Herbal Tablets.
description=[*]The mortar and pestle is kept and may take light wear.
description=[*]When added to an existing save, Gastro-Calm appears only in containers whose loot has not already been generated.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer and Languages[/h2]
description=Multiplayer is supported. Medicine use is validated by the server, and consumption, thirst, and treatment state are synchronized to the player.
description=
description=Includes full in-game text in English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.
description=
description=[h2]Important[/h2]
description=These medicines reduce Food Sickness and Poison. They do not cure the Knox Infection, wound infection, colds, or directly restore ordinary health.
description=
